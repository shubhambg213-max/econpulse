Weekly Wrap-up
Week of 19 – 25 August 2026
🔎 The week in one line: Indian equities logged a second straight weekly loss (Sensex −0.6% to 77,541, Nifty −0.5% to 24,252) as unusually hawkish RBI minutes — markets now price a possible October rate hike — pushed the 10-year G-sec to a two-month-high 6.87% and dragged the rupee to a record ~95.8/$, with Brent near a one-year high ~$94 on US-Iran/Hormuz tension adding to the squeeze; metals and private banks held up while IT, FMCG and autos lagged. Abroad, the S&P 500 fell 1.9% on the week (Nasdaq −2.5%) as US debt crossed $40tn and long-term Treasury yields surged (10Y ~4.7%, 30Y ~5.25%) despite Treasury Secretary Bessent doubling bond buybacks — attention now turns to the 28 Aug Jackson Hole symposium under Fed Chair Warsh, with September rate-hike odds easing to ~25%.
Monthly Wrap-up
Past 30 days · to 25 August 2026
🔎 The month in one line: India's price picture dominated — CPI re-accelerated to a 19-month-high 4.45% in July (from 4.38%) on oil and food; the RBI held repo at 5.25% for a fourth time on 5 Aug (FY27 growth 6.7%, inflation 5%) but its 20 Aug minutes turned notably hawkish, with markets pricing a possible October hike. Bank credit growth ran near a two-year high and Q1 FY27 earnings beat a feared decline as BFSI (+20%) and metals (+53%) led; economists now peg the 29 Aug Q1 GDP print at a four-quarter-low ~7%. Kharif acreage closed to ~1.5% below last year as an August monsoon rebound lifted planting. Firm crude and hawkish minutes kept the 10-year G-sec near 6.87% and the rupee near a record ~95.8/$, though Brent eased from a one-year high ~$94 to ~$92 late in the month; the Sensex steadied around 77,656 by 25 Aug. Abroad, a cool US July CPI powered the S&P 500 to records mid-month before surging long-term Treasury yields (10Y ~4.7%) — as US debt crossed $40tn — drove back-to-back weekly declines, near 7,650 by 24 Aug.
