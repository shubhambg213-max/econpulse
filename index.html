Weekly Wrap-up
Week of 25 – 31 August 2026
🔎 The week in one line: The week ended on a policy high but a market low. India's Q1 FY27 GDP, released 31 Aug, grew a stronger-than-expected 7.8% (beating the RBI's 7% view) on 9.2% manufacturing, 12.1% financial-real-estate-IT services and an 11.9% investment surge, even as farm growth cooled to 3.6% — yet equities fell the same day, the Sensex down 377 to 76,888 and the Nifty to 24,029 as metal, IT and realty dragged. The decisive swing was oil reversing course: after sliding below $90 on 28 Aug, Brent jumped back above $91 by 31 Aug as US-Iran strikes resumed near the Strait of Hormuz — a renewed hit to India's import bill that pushed the 10-year G-sec to ~6.95% (near a 16-month high) and kept the rupee near a record ~95.4/$ with the RBI intervening. Abroad, Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole debut on 28 Aug (inflation "above our 2% target") sent September rate-hike odds spiking to ~57% from ~35% and nudged the 10-year Treasury to 4.72%, dipping the S&P 500 to 7,712 — a sharp turn from the prior week's Nvidia-led AI optimism.
Monthly Wrap-up
Past 30 days · to 31 August 2026
🔎 The month in one line: The month closed with a growth surprise wrapped in an oil shock. India's Q1 FY27 GDP, out 31 Aug, printed a robust 7.8% — beating the RBI's 7% estimate on 9.2% manufacturing and 12.1% financial-real-estate-IT services, though decelerating from 8.6% the prior quarter as farm growth eased to 3.6%. It capped a month where CPI had re-accelerated to a 19-month-high 4.45% (from 4.38%) on oil and food; the RBI held repo at 5.25% for a fourth time on 5 Aug (FY27 growth 6.7%, inflation 5%) but its 20 Aug minutes turned notably hawkish. Bank credit ran near a two-year-high 18.6% and Q1 FY27 earnings beat a feared decline as BFSI (+20%) and metals (+53%) led; kharif acreage crossed 1,065 lakh ha, ~1.5% below last year. The defining late-month whipsaw was crude: Brent unwound its war premium below $90 around 28 Aug, then leapt back above $91 by 31 Aug as US-Iran strikes resumed near Hormuz — flipping relief into an import-bill risk that sent the 10-year G-sec to ~6.95% (16-month high) and knocked the Sensex to 76,888 with the rupee near a record ~95.4/$. Abroad, US debt crossed $40tn mid-month and Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole debut (28 Aug) spiked September rate-hike odds to ~57%, lifting the 10-year Treasury to 4.72% and dipping the S&P 500 to 7,712 after its earlier Nvidia-led AI rally.
