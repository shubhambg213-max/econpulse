Weekly Wrap-up
Week of 14 – 20 August 2026
🔎 The week in one line: Indian equities were choppy — a firmer Brent near $94 and a rupee pinned close to a record low (~95.6/$) drove a seven-session slide, before a sharp rebound on 20 Aug (Sensex +628 to 77,538, Nifty to 24,232) as US moves to contain surging global bond yields and a steadier rupee revived buying; media and realty led. The RBI ended its FCNR(B) forex-swap facility a month early after $56.85bn of inflows, keeping the 10-year G-sec around ~6.83% and weighing on bank stocks. On prices, July WPI eased to 9.78% even as CPI held at a 19-month-high 4.45%, keeping the RBI's neutral 5.25% stance in focus. Abroad, the S&P 500 steadied near 7,707 as Treasury buybacks eased yields, while the 19 Aug FOMC minutes revealed a hawkish split with three dissents favouring a rate hike.
Monthly Wrap-up
Past 30 days · to 20 August 2026
🔎 The month in one line: India's price picture dominated — CPI re-accelerated to a 19-month-high 4.45% in July (from 4.38%) on oil and food, even as WPI eased to 9.78% on softer fuel costs; the RBI held repo at 5.25% for a fourth time on 5 Aug (FY27 growth 6.7%, inflation 5%), bank credit growth hit a two-year high of 18.6%, and Q1 FY27 earnings beat a feared decline as BFSI (+20%) and metals (+53%) led. August rains narrowed the monsoon deficit to ~11.5%, though kharif acreage stayed below last year. A firm Brent near $94 and a rupee close to a record low (~95.6/$) drove a seven-session Sensex slide before a 628-point rebound to 77,538 on 20 Aug, with the 10-year G-sec around ~6.83% after the RBI ended its FCNR(B) forex-swap facility early. Abroad, a cool US July CPI powered the S&P 500 to a record 7,784 on 12 Aug; it steadied near 7,707 by 20 Aug, with the 19 Aug FOMC minutes showing three dissents for a rate hike.
