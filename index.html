Weekly Wrap-up
Week of 27 August – 2 September 2026
🔎 The week in one line: A growth surprise gave way to an oil-driven selloff. India's Q1 FY27 GDP, released 31 Aug, grew a stronger-than-expected 7.8% (beating the RBI's 7% view) on 9.2% manufacturing, 12.1% financial-real-estate-IT services and an 11.9% investment surge, even as farm growth cooled to 3.6% — yet equities then fell three straight sessions, the Sensex down to ~76,471 and the Nifty below 23,900 by 2 Sep. The decisive swing was crude: after slipping below $90 in late August, Brent surged back above $95 as US-Iran strikes escalated near the Strait of Hormuz — a renewed hit to India's import bill that pushed the 10-year G-sec briefly past 7% (near a 16-month high), though the rupee held firmer than August's ~95.4, near a two-month high around 94.9/$. Abroad, Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole stance kept September rate-hike odds near 57.5% and lifted the 10-year Treasury to 4.75% (highest since Jan 2025), dipping the S&P 500 to 7,686; the IMF held 2026 global growth at 3.0%, crediting AI investment for cushioning the war-and-trade drag.
Monthly Wrap-up
Past 30 days · to 2 September 2026
🔎 The month in one line: A growth surprise wrapped in an oil shock. India's Q1 FY27 GDP, out 31 Aug, printed a robust 7.8% — beating the RBI's 7% estimate on 9.2% manufacturing and 12.1% financial-real-estate-IT services, though decelerating from 8.6% the prior quarter as farm growth eased to 3.6%. It capped a month where CPI had re-accelerated to a 19-month-high 4.45% (from 4.38%) on oil and food; the RBI held repo at 5.25% for a fourth time on 5 Aug (FY27 growth raised to 6.7%) but its minutes turned notably hawkish. Bank credit ran near a two-year-high 18.6% and Q1 FY27 earnings beat a feared decline as BFSI (+20%) and metals (+53%) led; kharif acreage ended ~1.5–1.7% below last year with El Niño a lingering risk. The defining swing was crude: Brent unwound its war premium below $90 in late August, then surged back above $95 into early September as US-Iran strikes escalated near Hormuz — a ~13% monthly gain that flipped relief into an import-bill risk, pushed the 10-year G-sec briefly past 7% and dragged the Sensex under 77,000, though the rupee firmed to a two-month high near 94.9/$. Abroad, US debt crossed $40tn and Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole debut kept September rate-hike odds ~57%, lifting the 10-year Treasury to 4.75% and dipping the S&P 500 to 7,686; the IMF held global growth at 3.0%, crediting AI investment for offsetting war-and-trade headwinds.
