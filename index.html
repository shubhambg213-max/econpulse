Weekly Wrap-up
Week of 28 August – 3 September 2026
🔎 The week in one line: A growth surprise gave way to an oil-driven, four-session drift. India's Q1 FY27 GDP, released 31 Aug, grew a stronger-than-expected 7.8% (beating the RBI's 7% view) on 9.2% manufacturing, 12.1% financial-real-estate-IT services and an 11.9% investment surge, even as farm growth cooled to 3.6% — and August's services PMI rose to 54.1 with the fastest job creation in 15 months, yet equities still slipped four straight sessions, the Sensex to 76,153 and the Nifty to 23,873 by 3 Sep. The swing factor was crude: after slipping below $90 in late August, Brent swung back to $96–97 as US-Iran tensions persisted near the Strait of Hormuz — a renewed hit to India's import bill that pushed the 10-year G-sec briefly past 7% before easing to ~6.95%, while the rupee firmed to a two-month high near 94.4/$. Abroad, the 10-year Treasury hit 4.81% (highest since Nov 2023), but markets stayed split on a September Fed hike — pricing ~66% odds even as NY Fed's Williams saw "no clear signs" one is needed and Goldman called it very unlikely — and the S&P 500 rebounded to 7,662; the IMF held 2026 global growth at 3.0%, crediting AI investment for cushioning the war-and-trade drag.
Monthly Wrap-up
Past 30 days · to 3 September 2026
🔎 The month in one line: A growth surprise wrapped in an oil shock. India's Q1 FY27 GDP, out 31 Aug, printed a robust 7.8% — beating the RBI's 7% estimate on 9.2% manufacturing and 12.1% financial-real-estate-IT services, though decelerating from 8.6% the prior quarter as farm growth eased to 3.6%; August's services PMI then rose to 54.1 with the fastest hiring in 15 months, and GST receipts climbed ~14.8% to nearly ₹2 lakh crore. It capped a month where CPI had re-accelerated to a 19-month-high 4.45% (from 4.38%) on oil and food; the RBI held repo at 5.25% for a fourth time on 5 Aug (FY27 growth raised to 6.7%) but its minutes turned notably hawkish. Bank credit ran near a two-year-high 18.6% and Q1 FY27 earnings beat a feared decline as BFSI (+20%) and metals (+53%) led; kharif acreage ended ~1.5% below last year with El Niño a lingering risk. The defining swing was crude: Brent unwound its war premium below $90 in late August, then swung back to $96–97 into early September as US-Iran tensions persisted near Hormuz — a ~13% monthly gain that flipped relief into an import-bill risk, pushed the 10-year G-sec briefly past 7% and dragged the Sensex to 76,153, though the rupee firmed to a two-month high near 94.4/$. Abroad, US debt crossed $40tn and the 10-year Treasury hit 4.81% (highest since Nov 2023); markets split on a September Fed hike (~66% odds vs officials' and Goldman's caution) and the S&P 500 held near 7,662; the IMF kept global growth at 3.0%, crediting AI investment for offsetting war-and-trade headwinds.
