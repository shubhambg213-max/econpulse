Weekly Wrap-up
Week of 29 August – 4 September 2026
🔎 The week in one line: A four-session, oil-driven drift, then a relief bounce as the Fed narrative flipped from hikes to cuts. India's Q1 FY27 GDP (out 31 Aug) grew a stronger-than-expected 7.8% on 9.2% manufacturing and 12.1% financial-real-estate-IT services, and August's services PMI rose to 54.1 with the fastest hiring in 15 months, yet equities first slipped four straight sessions before the Sensex snapped the streak on 4 Sep to close at 76,515 (+363) and the Nifty at 23,898, led by metals. Crude was the earlier drag — Brent had swung back toward $97 on Strait of Hormuz supply fears — but eased to ~$95 into week-end, letting the 10-year G-sec ease from a near-3-month-high 6.98% and the rupee firm 22 paise to 94.51/$. The bigger shift came from the US: soft jobs data (ADP +38k in August, rising claims) and Fed Governor Waller backing a cut pushed markets toward a September Fed CUT, dragging the 10-year Treasury down to ~4.76% and lifting the S&P 500 to a record 7,747; the IMF held 2026 global growth at 3.0%, crediting AI investment for cushioning the war-and-trade drag. Friday's US non-farm payrolls loom as the key test.
Monthly Wrap-up
Past 30 days · to 4 September 2026
🔎 The month in one line: A growth surprise wrapped in an oil shock, ending with a dovish turn abroad. India's Q1 FY27 GDP, out 31 Aug, printed a robust 7.8% — beating the RBI's 7% estimate on 9.2% manufacturing and 12.1% financial-real-estate-IT services, though decelerating from 8.6% as farm growth eased to 3.6%; August's services PMI rose to 54.1 with the fastest hiring in 15 months, and GST receipts climbed ~14.8% to nearly ₹2 lakh crore, even as the Q1 current-account deficit widened to $4.2bn (0.5% of GDP). CPI had re-accelerated to a 19-month-high 4.45% on oil and food; the RBI held repo at 5.25% a fourth time on 5 Aug. Bank credit ran near a two-year-high 18.6% and Q1 earnings beat a feared decline as BFSI (+20%) and metals (+53%) led; kharif acreage ended ~1.5% below last year with El Niño a lingering risk. Crude drove the swings — Brent dipped below $90, spiked back toward $97 on Hormuz tensions, then eased to ~$95 by 4 Sep as throughput stayed choked but demand signals softened. The late-month story was the US: soft jobs data and Fed Governor Waller backing a cut flipped the debate from a September hike to a September CUT, dragging the 10-year Treasury from a Nov-2023-high 4.81% down to ~4.76%, lifting the S&P 500 to a record 7,747 and letting the rupee firm to 94.51/$ and the 10-year G-sec ease from ~6.98%. The IMF kept global growth at 3.0%, crediting AI investment for offsetting war-and-trade headwinds.
