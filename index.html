Weekly Wrap-up
Week of 13 – 19 August 2026
🔎 The week in one line: Markets stayed on the back foot — a firmer Brent above $91 and a rupee pinned near a record low (~95.77/$) dragged the Sensex down to 76,910 and the Nifty to 24,078 by 19 Aug, a seventh straight fall, with energy and FMCG the weakest and IT the lone gainer on rupee weakness. The RBI ended its FCNR(B) forex-swap facility a month early after $56.85bn of inflows, nudging the 10-year G-sec up to ~6.84% and weighing on bank stocks. On prices, July WPI eased to 9.78% even as CPI held at a 19-month-high 4.45%, keeping the RBI's neutral 5.25% stance in focus. Abroad, an AI- and semiconductor-led selloff (Nvidia, Micron, Broadcom) and rising Treasury yields pulled the S&P 500 down to 7,692 by 18 Aug, while soft US retail sales trimmed September Fed-hike odds to ~30%.
Monthly Wrap-up
Past 30 days · to 19 August 2026
🔎 The month in one line: India's price picture dominated — CPI re-accelerated to a 19-month-high 4.45% in July (from 4.38%) on oil and food, even as WPI eased to 9.78% on softer fuel costs; the RBI held repo at 5.25% for a fourth time on 5 Aug (FY27 growth 6.7%, inflation 5%), bank credit growth hit a two-year high of 18.6%, and Q1 FY27 earnings beat a feared decline as BFSI (+20%) and metals (+53%) led. August rains narrowed the kharif sowing gap, though acreage stayed ~2% below last year at 1,016.6 lh by 14 Aug. Yet a firm Brent above $91 and a rupee near a record low (~95.77/$) dragged the Sensex to 76,910 by 19 Aug in a seventh straight fall and lifted the 10-year G-sec to ~6.84% after the RBI ended its FCNR(B) forex-swap facility a month early. Abroad, a cool US July CPI powered the S&P 500 to a record 7,784 on 12 Aug before an AI- and chip-led selloff pulled it back to 7,692 by 18 Aug.
