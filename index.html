Weekly Wrap-up
Week of 15 – 21 August 2026
🔎 The week in one line: Indian equities logged a second straight weekly loss (Sensex −0.6% to 77,541, Nifty −0.5% to 24,252) as unusually hawkish RBI minutes — markets now price a possible October rate hike — pushed the 10-year G-sec to a two-month-high 6.87% and dragged the rupee to a record ~95.8/$, with Brent near a one-year high ~$94 on US-Iran/Hormuz tension adding to the squeeze; metals and private banks held up while IT, FMCG and autos lagged. Monthly Wrap-up
Past 30 days · to 21 August 2026
🔎 The month in one line: India's price picture dominated — CPI re-accelerated to a 19-month-high 4.45% in July (from 4.38%) on oil and food, even as WPI eased to 9.78% on softer fuel costs; the RBI held repo at 5.25% for a fourth time on 5 Aug (FY27 growth 6.7%, inflation 5%) but its 20 Aug minutes turned notably hawkish, with markets now pricing a possible October hike. A firm Brent near $94 and hawkish minutes drove the 10-year G-sec to 6.87% and the rupee to a record ~95.8/$, leaving the Sensex with back-to-back weekly losses at 77,541. Abroad, a cool US July CPI powered the S&P 500 to records mid-month before a surge in long-term Treasury yields (10Y ~4.7%) — as US debt crossed $40tn — pulled it into two straight weekly declines, near 7,674 by 21 Aug.
